🧭 Using A.L.E.X. and the helpdesks

Asking A.L.E.X. a question

In PIR's Discord, type /ask followed by your question. A.L.E.X. answers only from PIR's own published materials and lists its sources. Add private:true to see the answer only yourself. A.L.E.X. cannot read your chats, does not give medical, dosing, or legal advice, and is not a crisis line.

Which helpdesk should I use?

  • /litcom — the Literature Committee: literature requests, corrections, translations, and ideas.
  • /techcom — the Tech Committee: the website, email, forms, Zoom, and general tech problems.
  • /github — PIR's GitHub organization: repositories, builds, GitHub Pages, and Actions.
  • /pr — the Public Relations Committee: social media, press and outreach, brand assets (logos), and the De Vine News newsletter (a PR subcommittee).
  • /mod — Discord moderation, privately. You can also right-click any message and choose Apps → Report to mods.

Opening a ticket

Type the desk command, choose Problem or request or Idea or feedback, and fill in the short form. A private thread opens between you and that committee — only you, the committee, and server moderators can see it. You'll get a ticket number like TECH-12. Check on it any time with /ticket TECH-12.

Signing in on the web portals with a magic link

The helpdesk web portals let people who don't use Discord open and follow tickets. There is no password. Enter your email address and PIR sends you a one-time sign-in link. The link works once and expires after 15 minutes; if it expired, just request a new one. After signing in you stay signed in on that browser for 7 days, or sign out any time. Your email is used only to send updates about your tickets, and you can ask helpdesk@psychedelicsinrecovery.org to delete it. Discord users can choose "Sign in with Discord" instead.

Privacy

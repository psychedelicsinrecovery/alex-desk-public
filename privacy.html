🔐 Privacy notice: A.L.E.X. & helpdesks

The detailed, step-by-step notice for PIR's Discord helper and helpdesks. PIR's org-wide privacy policy (websites, email, store, AI helpers) is at psychedelicsinrecovery/.github/privacy-policy.md. Questions or deletion requests: helpdesk@psychedelicsinrecovery.org.

What A.L.E.X. can and can't see

  • A.L.E.X. runs as a Discord application (HTTP interactions), not a user account.
  • It can't read channels, threads or DMs. It only receives what you type into its own commands (/ask, /litcom, /techcom, /github, /mod, …), forms and buttons.
  • With every interaction, Discord sends A.L.E.X. your user ID, server and channel IDs, your roles in that server, and the text you entered.

Step by step

/ask

  1. A safety check runs first, on the text alone.
    • If it sounds like a crisis, you immediately get a private message with crisis resources. No AI is involved.
    • If PIR has set up a support role, its members get a notice in a private channel that you may need support. What you wrote is never included.
    • If it asks for dosing, sourcing or medical advice, you get a fixed, kind refusal.
  2. Otherwise, your question is matched against PIR's public materials.
  3. The question text and the matching excerpts go to an AI model to write a sourced answer: during our pilot, open models hosted by NVIDIA; once approved by TechCom, Anthropic's Claude (API, which Anthropic does not use for training by default). Your name and Discord ID are never sent to the model.
  4. The answer appears in the channel (or privately, if you chose private).
  5. Nothing you typed is stored. We keep only anonymous counts, such as "a question was answered" or "👍".

Helpdesk tickets

  1. You fill in a short form: subject, details, urgency.
  2. A private thread opens that only you, the committee team and server moderators can see.
  3. For committee desks, a record is also created in that committee's private GitHub repository:
    • your subject, details and urgency;
    • a link to the thread;
    • a pseudonymous tag derived from your Discord ID with a secret key. The raw ID is never written there.
  4. Moderation reports stay inside Discord. They're never copied to GitHub.
  5. To check status, /ticket <id> works only for you and the committee.

Web portal sign-in (when available)

  • Discord sign-in: we receive only your Discord ID and username.
  • Email sign-in: we email a one-time link (valid 15 minutes, usable once), sent by Brevo (PIR's email provider; Resend as backup). We keep your email only to send updates about your tickets. Sessions last 7 days.

Retention & your rights

  • /ask: nothing stored.
  • Tickets: kept while useful to the committee, then archived.
  • You can ask for your tickets to be deleted at any time: helpdesk@psychedelicsinrecovery.org.

Not a crisis service

A.L.E.X. is an automated helper. In an emergency, contact local emergency services. In the US, call or text 988.
